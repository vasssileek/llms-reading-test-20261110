What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card that's built directly into your smartphone, tablet, or smartwatch. Unlike traditional physical SIM cards that you insert into your device, an eSIM is a programmable chip that allows you to activate a cellular plan without needing a physical card. This technology makes it incredibly easy for travelers to get connected instantly in Japan without having to find a local store or deal with tiny SIM card trays.

With an eSIM for Japan, you can purchase and activate your data plan entirely online. Simply buy a plan, receive a QR code via email, scan it with your device, and you're connected to Japan's cellular networks within minutes. No more airport kiosks, no more language barriers, and no more worrying about losing tiny plastic cards during your travels.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I activate my Japan eSIM?▼

Your Japan eSIM can be activated instantly upon purchase. You'll receive your QR code within seconds. The actual network connection happens automatically when you land in Japan and turn on your device.

When does my eSIM validity period start?▼

Your eSIM validity period starts only when you first connect to the network in Japan, not at the moment of purchase. This means you can buy your eSIM in advance without worrying about it expiring before your trip. The validity countdown begins only when you actually start using it.

Does this work with my iPhone/Android?▼

Yes! Our eSIM works with all unlocked, eSIM-compatible devices including iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, and many other modern smartphones. Check your device settings for "eSIM" or "Add Cellular Plan" to confirm compatibility.

Can I keep my regular phone number?▼

Absolutely! Your eSIM works alongside your regular SIM card. You can receive calls and texts on your home number while using the eSIM for data in Japan. This dual-SIM functionality means you stay connected without swapping SIM cards.

What happens if I run out of data?▼

For fixed GB plans, if you use all your data before your plan expires, you can easily purchase additional data or a new plan directly from eSIM.dog. For unlimited plans, you won't run out - instead, you'll get high-speed data up to your daily limit, then reduced speeds for the rest of the day (the daily limit resets every 24 hours). There's no penalty for either - simply choose what works best for you.

Is there a speed limit on these plans?▼

Fixed GB plans marked "Full-speed data" have no artificial speed limits - you'll get full 5G/LTE speeds depending on network availability in Japan. Plans marked "1 Mbps max" are speed-capped, and "No hotspot" means tethering is unavailable. Unlimited plans offer high-speed data up to your chosen daily limit, with reduced speeds after (which you can select before purchase). Both plan types provide the same quality networks that local Japan residents enjoy.
